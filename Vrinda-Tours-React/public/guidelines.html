Pilgrim Guidelines & Temple Etiquette

Practical advice and sacred protocols to ensure a blissful and respectful yatra across Brij Dham.

🥻

1. Temple Dress Code & Modesty

Brij temples are revered holy sanctuaries. Devotees are requested to dress respectfully:

  • Recommended Attire: Traditional Indian wear (Kurta-Pyjama/Dhoti for men; Saree, Salwar Kameez, or modest long attire for women).
  • Footwear: All footwear must be deposited at designated Chappal stands before entering temple gates.
  • Leather Items: Belts, wallets, or bags made of genuine animal leather are prohibited inside inner temple sanctums.
👓

2. Monkey Safety & Eyewear Precautions

Old Vrindavan alleys, Banke Bihari Marg, and Barsana Hill are home to agile monkeys:

  • Remove Eyeglasses & Sunglasses: Store glasses, specs, and shades securely inside zipped bags before entering old temple lanes.
  • Avoid Visible Food or Plastic Bags: Keep prasad and offerings enclosed in cloth jhola bags.
  • In Case of Snatching: Do not attack or corner the monkey; local shopkeepers frequently assist in offering juice/frooti in exchange for returned items.
⚠️ Important: Never hold smartphones loosely at arm's length while walking through Banke Bihari or Nidhivan alleys.
👣

3. Govardhan 21 km Parikrama Guidelines

Circumambulating the sacred Giriraj Maharaj is a pinnacle of devotion:

  • Barefoot vs. E-Rickshaw: Devotees can walk barefoot or take authorized battery-operated E-Rickshaws via Vrinda Vihar.
  • Starting Points: Most yatris begin at Manasi Ganga or Dan Ghati Mandir.
  • Sacred Rule: Always circumambulate in a clockwise direction (Pradakshina), keeping Giriraj Hill to your right shoulder.
  • Hydration: Drink plenty of water and buttermilk (Chhaachh) available along the well-shaded parikrama marg.
🥗

4. Sattvic Food & Prasadam Standards

Brij Dham strictly observes Vaishnava dietary ethics:

  • Non-vegetarian food, eggs, alcohol, and smoking are strictly forbidden throughout sacred Vrindavan, Govardhan, and Barsana.
  • Traditional sattvic bhojanalayas prepare food without onion (pyaz) or garlic (lahsun).
  • Always accept temple Mahaprasadam with both hands and consume with devotion.
