Sacred Mission & Heritage

Preserving the Eternal Pilgrimage of Brij Mandal

Vrinda Vihar was founded by Vrindopnishad to empower pilgrims with pure devotion, zero-middleman transparency, and authentic local Brajwasi hospitality.

Our Divine Origin

Brij Mandal — spanning Vrindavan, Mathura, Barsana, Govardhan, Gokul, and Nandgaon — has welcomed saints, devotees, and spiritual seekers for millennia. However, modern commercialization, touts, opaque taxi fares, and unverified accommodations often distract pilgrims from their sacred sadhana.

Vrinda Vihar bridges timeless spiritual devotion with modern technology. We provide a single, trustworthy digital sanctuary where devotees can book verified electric rickshaws, stay in sacred ashrams, savor authentic sattvic prasadam, and circumambulate the 84 Kos Dham with peace of mind.
