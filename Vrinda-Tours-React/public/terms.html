Policy Framework

Service Terms & Devotee Agreement

Clear rules ensuring smooth, fair, and reverent operations.

Clause I

1. Transparent Fare Policy

All estimated fares displayed in the app are all-inclusive of standard travel, fuel, and driver seva charges. Toll taxes or special state entry fees (if applicable for interstate border crossings into Rajasthan or Haryana) are clearly itemized before booking confirmation with zero hidden platform markups.

Clause II

2. Cancellation & Free Rescheduling

Devotees can reschedule trip dates or time slots at zero extra penalty up to 2 hours prior to scheduled departure. In case of unexpected vehicle breakdown or severe monsoon weather in Brij Dham, a replacement vehicle is dispatched immediately at no additional charge.

Clause III

3. Devotee Code of Conduct

As Brij Dham is a world-revered spiritual sanctuary, carrying intoxicants, alcohol, tobacco, or non-vegetarian substances inside partner vehicles is strictly prohibited. Smoking is banned across all partner fleets to preserve the sanctity of the yatra.

Clause IV

4. Driver Partner Fair Charter & Grievance

Every Brajwasi driver is treated with dignifying respect as a servitor (Sarathi) of the holy Dham. Drivers receive 100% of their earned fare without deduction, and are guaranteed regulated driving hours for passenger safety. For disputes, contact our grievance team at support@vrindopnishad.in.

The Vrindopnishad Fair Fare Pledge: Spiritual seva must never be compromised by greed. We believe every Brajwasi driver deserves honorable compensation and every pilgrim deserves honest, peaceful, and uncompromised holy travel.
