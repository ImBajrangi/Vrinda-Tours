1. Platform Scope & Services

Vrinda Vihar provides digital mediation, route discovery, and direct booking facilitation connecting devotees with pre-verified local Brajwasi service providers:

  • Sarathi Driver Mobility: Instant electric rickshaw & taxi dispatch across Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan, and Barsana.
  • Ashram & Hotel Stays: Room reservations at verified temple guesthouses and hotels.
  • Sattvic Dining: Pure vegetarian, Vaishnava-compliant prasadam and restaurant menus.
  • 84 Kos Yatra Concierge: Guided itinerary packages and local driver escorts.

2. Transparent Fare Policy & Direct Devotee Billing

  • All estimated ride fares shown on the platform are standard fixed rates established with local driver associations.
  • Devotees pay drivers directly in cash or through QR UPI upon trip completion.
  • Vrinda Vihar charges 0% booking commission to devotees.

3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Ride Requests: Free cancellation at any time before driver arrival. No cancellation penalties.

  • In the event of temple closures, inclement weather, or sudden local road restrictions, our support concierge assists in free rescheduling.
