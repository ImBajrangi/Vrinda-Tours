Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my assigned Sarathi E-Rickshaw?

When you book a ride on Vrinda Vihar, a real-time floating activity capsule displays your driver's name, mobile number, vehicle number, and live GPS location on the map.

How do I cancel or modify an ashram stay?

You can cancel or reschedule with 0 penalties up to 24 hours prior by contacting our 24/7 WhatsApp concierge or replying to your confirmation voucher.

What should I do if a driver asks for excess fare?

All Vrinda Vihar fares are fixed and pre-agreed. If you encounter any dispute, share your ride ID with our support line (+91 76182 18181) for immediate resolution.
