Help Centre FAQ

Got questions? We're here to help.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booked driver?
You can cancel any trip free of charge up to 30 minutes before pickup directly inside the app, or message our WhatsApp concierge at +91 76182 18181 for instant modifications.
Are payment transactions safe on Vrinda Vihar?
All online UPI and card transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL banking security. Cash and direct UPI to the driver upon trip completion are also fully supported with zero platform convenience surcharges.
