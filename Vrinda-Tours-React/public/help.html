Support FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications on booking modifications, payments, and transit delays.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booked driver?
You can cancel any trip free of charge up to 30 minutes before pickup directly inside the app, or message our WhatsApp concierge at +91 76182 18181 for instant modifications.
Are payment transactions safe on Vrinda Vihar?
All online UPI and card transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL banking security. Direct UPI to the driver upon trip completion is also fully supported with zero platform convenience surcharges.
What should I do if my train is delayed arriving at Mathura Junction?
Simply message your driver or the 24/7 Operations Desk on WhatsApp with your updated PNR or estimated arrival time. Your assigned Brajwasi driver will adjust pickup time without extra waiting penalty.
