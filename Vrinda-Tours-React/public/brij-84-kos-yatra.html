Spiritual Significance

Why is Brij 84 Kos Parikrama Revered?

The eternal playground of Shri Radha and Krishna where every particle of dust (Brij Raj) is worshipable.

✨
Padma Purana Quotation: "Whoever circumambulates Mathura Mandala during the month of Purushottam (Adhik Maas) or Kartik attains the supreme lotus feet of Lord Govinda, destroying millions of lifetimes of sins."
Sacred Geography

The 12 Holy Forests (Dvadasa Vans)

Divided across the eastern and western banks of the divine Yamuna river.

Forest 1
🌺

1. Madhuvan

Where Dhruva Maharaja meditated on Lord Vishnu and achieved darshan in 6 months. Also the site where Lord Shatrughna defeated Lavanasura.

Forest 2
☀️

2. Talavan

The orchard filled with palm fruits where Lord Balarama defeated the donkey demon Dhenukasura, freeing the forest for cowherd boys.

Forest 3
🪷

3. Kumudvan

Adorned with fragrant white lotus flowers (Kumuda). A tranquil retreat where Krishna and Balarama played water sports with Gopis.

Forest 4
🦢

4. Bahulavan

Named after the faithful cow Bahula. Site of Man Sarovar, where Shri Radha Rani withdrew in divine transcendental sulk (Maan Lila).

Forest 5
⛰️

5. Kamyavan

The forest that fulfills all divine desires (Kama). Houses 84 sacred Kunds, Charan Pahadi, and the ancient temple of Shri Vrinda Devi.

Forest 6
🦚

6. Khadiravan

Where Lord Krishna killed the gigantic crane demon Bakasura. Contains Sangam Kund and sacred acacia groves where cows grazed peacefully.

Forest 7
🌿

7. Vrindavan

The heart of Brij Dham, dedicated to Tulasi Devi (Vrinda). Site of Maha Raas Lila, Seva Kunj, Nidhivan, and thousands of Radha Krishna temples.

Forest 8
🌳

8. Bhandirvan

Home of the colossal Bhandirvat banyan tree where Brahma ji personally performed the cosmic Gandharva wedding of Shri Radha and Krishna.

Forest 9
🕊️

9. Bhadravan

Located on the eastern bank of Yamuna. Filled with Bhadra (auspicious) trees where Krishna and Balarama shared butter milk and lunch.

Forest 10
🐄

10. Mahavan (Gokul)

Where Nanda Baba's palace stood and Krishna spent his tender childhood. Sites include Brahmand Ghat, Chaurasi Khamba, and Ukhal Bandhan Lila.

Forest 11
🌾

11. Lohavan

Where the iron-bodied demon Lohasura was vanquished. Rich in mineral springs and sacred kadamba trees.

Forest 12
🌅

12. Bilvavan

Filled with sacred Bel trees. Where Goddess Lakshmi performed thousands of years of tapasya desiring to participate in Krishna's Raas Lila.

Travel Plan

7-Day Recommended Yatra Itinerary

Optimized for senior citizens, families, and solo devotees with minimal fatigue.

Day 1

Mathura & Sacred Ghats

Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, Dwarkadhish Mandir, Vishram Ghat evening Yamuna Maha Aarti, and Madhuvan darshan.

Day 2

Mahavan, Gokul & Rawal

Chaurasi Khamba, Raman Reti, Brahmand Ghat, and Rawal Dham (the sacred birthplace of Shri Radha Rani).

Day 3

Vrindavan Central Darshan

Shri Bankey Bihari Mangala darshan, Radha Raman Ji, Radha Damodar, Seva Kunj, Nidhivan, and Yamuna boat parikrama.

Day 4

Bhandirvan, Belvan & Mansarovar

The cosmic wedding tree at Bhandirvan, Lakshmi Ji taposthali at Belvan, and tranquil Man Sarovar.

Day 5

Govardhan 21 km Giriraj Parikrama

Dan Ghati, Radha Kund, Shyam Kund, Kusum Sarovar, Jatipura Mukharbind, and Manasi Ganga snan.

Day 6

Barsana & Nandgaon Dham

Shri Radha Rani Mandir (Bhanugarh), Rangeeli Mahal, Mor Kuti, Nand Bhavan, and Pavana Sarovar.

Day 7

Kamyavan & Kokilavan

Chaurasi Kund parikrama in Kamyavan, Charan Pahadi, and Shani Dev temple with Kokila cuckoo grove.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential answers for planning your sacred pilgrimage.

What is the best time of year to undertake the 84 Kos Yatra?
The most auspicious and comfortable months are October through March (Kartik Maas and winter season) when temperatures are pleasant. Adhik Maas (Purushottam Month) every three years also draws millions of devotees.
Can senior citizens complete the Parikrama comfortably?
Yes! While traditional devotees walk on foot over 21-45 days, our platform provides specialized E-Rickshaws and comfortable AC vehicles with verified Brajwasi drivers who cater specifically to senior devotees and ensure step-free access wherever possible.
