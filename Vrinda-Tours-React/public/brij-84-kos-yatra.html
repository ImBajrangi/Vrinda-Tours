The Sacred Brij 84 Kos Yatra

Circumambulate the transcendental 84 Kos (approx. 250 km) holy realm of Shri Radha Krishna across 12 sacred forests, 24 upavans, and over 1,000 holy kunds.

What is Brij 84 Kos Yatra?

The Brij 84 Kos Yatra (Chaurasi Kos Parikrama) is one of the most revered pilgrimages in Sanatana Dharma. According to the Varaha Purana and Skanda Purana, performing the 84 Kos Parikrama of Brij Mandal grants the spiritual merit of visiting all sacred tirthas across the universe.

The 12 Sacred Forests (Vans of Braj)

The yatra traverses 12 sacred forests on both the eastern and western banks of holy Yamuna Ji:

1. Vrindavan The forest of holy Tulsi Devi, Maharaas leela & Sapta Devalaya temples.
2. Madhuvan Site of Dhruva Maharaj's penance & Shatrughna Ji's victory over Lavanasura.
3. Talavan Where Lord Balarama defeated the Dhenukasura demon in palm groves.
4. Kumudvan Sacred lotus groves where Krishna enjoyed divine water pastimes.
5. Bahulavan The forest honoring Bahula cow's divine truthfulness and devotion.
6. Kamyavan The wish-fulfilling forest filled with 84 sacred kunds and Charanamrit.
7. Khadiravan Where Lord Krishna protected the cows and defeated Bakasura.
8. Bhadravan Serene forest across Yamuna blessed by Lord Balarama.
9. Bhandirvan The transcendental banyan tree where Brahma Ji conducted Radha-Krishna's Gandharva marriage.
10. Belvan Where Goddess Lakshmi performed deep tapasya to witness Krishna's Raas Leela.
11. Lohavan Forest where Krishna pastured calves and subdued Lohasura.
12. Mahavan (Gokul) The ancient palace of Nanda Maharaj, Putana Moksha & Ukhal Bandhan leela.

Suggested Day-by-Day Itineraries

Day 1: Mathura & Gokul Dham

Shri Krishna Janmasthan, Dwarkadhish Temple, Vishram Ghat Yamuna Aarti, Raman Reti Gokul, Chaurasi Khamba, and Brahmand Ghat.

Day 2: Vrindavan Darshan & Alleys

Banke Bihari Ji morning darshan, Shri Radha Raman, Radha Vallabh, Nidhivan, Sevakunj, and evening Prem Mandir musical fountain show.

Day 3: Govardhan 21 km Giriraj Parikrama

Manasi Ganga, Dan Ghati, Radha Kund, Shyam Kund, Kusum Sarovar, Jatipura Mukharbind, and Puchhari Ka Lautha.

Day 4: Barsana & Nandgaon Dham

Shri Radha Rani Mandir on Bhanugarh hill, Rangeeli Mahal, Gahvar Van, Prem Sarovar, and Nand Bhawan at Nandgaon.
