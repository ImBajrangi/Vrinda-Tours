Privacy Policy &
Devotee Data Protection

At Vrinda Vihar, we treat your personal data with the same sanctity and respect as your spiritual pilgrimage. Read how we protect your location and personal information.

Last Updated: September 4, 2026 • Version 3.2
📍

Foreground-Only GPS

Location data is accessed exclusively while actively requesting rides or viewing nearest sacred temples on the live map. Never tracked in background.

🔒

256-Bit SSL Encryption

All authentication tokens, payment details, and contact numbers are transmitted over enterprise-grade HTTPS TLS 1.3 encrypted tunnels.

🛡️

Zero Data Commercialization

We strictly never sell, rent, or trade your travel logs, phone numbers, or yatra itineraries to third-party advertisers or marketing agencies.

1. Information We Collect

When using Vrinda Vihar services, we only collect essential data required to facilitate safe pilgrimage travel:

  • ✓
    Identity Data: Full Name and Mobile Number (for booking confirmation and driver coordination).
  • ✓
    Precise Location Data: Real-time latitude and longitude when matching you with the nearest Brajwasi E-Rickshaw driver.
  • ✓
    Transaction Records: Razorpay / UPI transaction reference IDs for ride and ashram booking confirmations.

2. Driver Verification & KYC

Every partner driver registered on Vrinda Vihar submits Government Aadhaar and Police Verification credentials. Driver location is shared in real time only during an active assigned ride.

3. Data Retention & Erasure Rights

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, you have the right to request full erasure of your account, ride history, and stored preferences at any time by emailing privacy@vrindopnishad.in.
