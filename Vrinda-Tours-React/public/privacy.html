Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026 • Compliant with India DPDP Act 2023

Vrinda Vihar ("we", "our", or "us"), operated under Vrindopnishad Trust, is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of devotees, pilgrims, drivers, and partners visiting our spiritual platform at to.vrindopnishad.in.

Core Privacy Commitment: We never sell, rent, or commercialize your personal information. GPS location is used exclusively during active navigation and ride dispatch within the Brij Mandal region.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device & GPS Location: Real-time device coordinates (collected only with explicit user permission) to locate nearby temples, display distance ETA, and match with nearby verified Sarathi drivers.
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number and name provided during ride dispatch, ashram reservation, or partner onboarding.
  • Payment Details: Transaction IDs and payment confirmation status. All sensitive card/UPI data is processed securely through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways (Stripe & NPCI UPI).
  • Technical Data: Browser type, operating system, and IP address for security audits, rate-limiting, and fraud prevention.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data strictly for legitimate spiritual and logistical services:

  • Matching devotees with local electric rickshaw drivers for temple darshan and parikrama.
  • Confirming room reservations at verified ashrams and guesthouses.
  • Providing live GPS navigation and route guidance across 84 Kos Yatra trails.
  • Responding to 24/7 devotee support inquiries and emergency assistance requests.

3. Location Data & Background Tracking

For pilgrims, location tracking is strictly foreground-only and terminates immediately when navigation or ride booking completes. For verified Sarathi driver partners, background location tracking is active only when toggled "Online" to receive nearby ride requests.

4. Data Protection & Security Measures

All data transmissions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS protocol. User records and database collections are protected by strict row-level security (RLS) rules ensuring only authorized platform administrators and verified partners can view relevant operational logs.

5. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

Devotees and partners retain full control over their personal information:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete summary of your personal data stored on Vrinda Vihar.
  • Right to Correction: Update outdated contact details, phone numbers, or vehicle registration records.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and ride history by emailing our support desk.
