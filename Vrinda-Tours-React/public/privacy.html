Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Vrindopnishad ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a sacred pilgrimage and travel platform serving the Braj Mandal region — including Vrindavan, Mathura, Barsana, Govardhan, and surrounding dhams. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our web application at to.vrindopnishad.in.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and phone number when you register or sign in.
  • Authentication data: Sign-in method (email/password or Google OAuth) via Supabase Auth.
  • Booking information: Travel preferences, destination, check-in/check-out dates entered in booking forms.
  • Location data: GPS coordinates when you use the map navigation feature (only with your explicit permission).
  • Usage data: Pages visited, filters applied, and interactions within the app (via Firebase Analytics).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session
  • To autofill booking forms and save your preferences
  • To show you relevant pilgrimage destinations and yatra packages
  • To send booking confirmations and driver arrival alerts via WhatsApp
  • To improve our app experience through anonymised analytics

3. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication data is stored securely in Supabase (EU/US data centres with AES-256 encryption). Location and booking preferences are stored in Google Firebase Firestore with Firestore security rules. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — Authentication & session management (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase — Database, analytics, and hosting (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google OAuth — Social sign-in (Google Privacy Policy)
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — Map tiles (no personal data shared)
  • DiceBear — Avatar generation using your name as seed (no data retained)

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time
  • Withdraw location permission via your browser settings
  • Request account deletion by contacting us
  • Use the app in Guest mode without creating an account

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to cache your session, search preferences, and favourite destinations for a faster experience. No tracking cookies are used. You can clear this data at any time via your browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us for immediate removal.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify registered users of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
